This contemporary residence combines clean architectural lines with textured finishes and warm timber accents. A striking façade introduces light-filled interiors, where soaring ceilings, expansive windows and a geometric timber display wall create a distinctive sense of space and character.

A covered alfresco area with a built-in barbecue provides an inviting setting for outdoor dining, while the swimming pool and leafy surroundings complete the home’s relaxed atmosphere. Thoughtful detailing throughout balances modern style with everyday comfort.
