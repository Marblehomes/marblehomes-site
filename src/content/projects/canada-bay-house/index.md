This contemporary two-storey residence combines sculptural design with warm, natural finishes. A sweeping curved staircase creates a striking centrepiece, complemented by timber cabinetry, elegant stone surfaces and soft ambient lighting. A curved kitchen island adds character while providing a practical space for everyday living.

Expansive glazing connects the interiors to a covered alfresco area with a timber-lined ceiling, while a sleek swimming pool complements the home’s clean architectural lines. Thoughtful detailing throughout creates a refined balance of style, comfort and functionality.
