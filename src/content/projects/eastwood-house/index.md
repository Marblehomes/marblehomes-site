Defined by an angular roofline and contrasting exterior finishes, this contemporary residence embraces its leafy setting. Expansive glazing fills the living spaces with natural light, while timber accents and a sleek kitchen with a generous island bring warmth and elegance to the interiors.

Large sliding doors connect the home to covered entertaining terraces with glass balustrades and elevated treetop views. Statement lighting and textured feature walls add character, creating a welcoming home for relaxed living and entertaining.
