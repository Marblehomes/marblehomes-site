Built by our team, this two-storey home combines a striking façade with bright, welcoming interiors. Contrasting exterior finishes and glass-fronted balconies create a strong street presence, while timber flooring, warm kitchen cabinetry and pendant lighting bring comfort and character inside.

The covered alfresco area provides an inviting space for outdoor entertaining. A finely detailed staircase and an elegant bathroom with marble-look finishes, a floating vanity and a freestanding bath showcase our attention to detail throughout the build.
