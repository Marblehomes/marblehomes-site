Grand proportions and classic detailing give this home a timeless presence. Delivered by our team, the residence features a soaring entrance, a sweeping timber staircase and decorative wall panelling, complemented by herringbone flooring and an elegant kitchen with a generous island.

Bathrooms pair fluted timber vanities with veined surfaces and arched mirrors. Expansive glass doors open onto covered entertaining areas overlooking the swimming pool, spa and landscaped lawn, creating a generous setting for relaxed family living.
