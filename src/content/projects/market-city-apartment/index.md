A refined interior transformation brings fresh elegance to this apartment renovation. A sweeping staircase with decorative balustrades and sculptural pendant lighting forms a striking focal point, while the updated kitchen pairs dark cabinetry with a curved island and subtle metallic accents.

Herringbone timber flooring adds warmth to the bedrooms, complemented by soft neutral tones. Renovated bathrooms feature floating vanities, illuminated mirrors and carefully selected finishes, creating a cohesive balance of classic character and modern comfort.
