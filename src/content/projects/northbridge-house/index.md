Warm timber tones, soft curves and elegant stone textures bring a distinctive character to this home. Arched openings and herringbone flooring complement bespoke cabinetry, while an illuminated curved staircase with decorative balustrades creates a striking focal point.

Expansive glazing opens the living spaces towards the swimming pool, creating an inviting connection outdoors. A sleek fireplace, timber-lined kitchen ceiling and refined bathroom finishes complete the home’s warm and sophisticated atmosphere.
