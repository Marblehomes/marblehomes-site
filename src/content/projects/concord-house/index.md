Sculptural curves and striking lighting define this contemporary home. A sweeping staircase with timber treads brings warmth to the interiors, while a double-height void and cascading pendant lights create an impressive sense of space.

Circular skylights introduce natural light, complementing bathrooms with richly veined stone finishes and soft concealed lighting. A distinctive powder room pairs muted green walls with a statement vanity and decorative mirror, adding character to the home’s refined palette.
