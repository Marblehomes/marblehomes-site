This contemporary residence combines a crisp geometric façade with bright interiors and warm timber accents. A welcoming entry features built-in bench seating and a striking veined feature wall, leading into inviting living and dining spaces. The kitchen pairs sleek white cabinetry with a curved island and subtle fluted detailing.

Elegant bathrooms continue the palette with timber vanities, arched mirrors and softly glowing wall lights. Natural textures and thoughtful joinery create a fresh, refined home designed for everyday comfort.
