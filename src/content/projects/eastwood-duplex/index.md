This contemporary residence pairs crisp white architectural forms with warm brickwork and timber accents. A stylish kitchen features a generous island, rich-toned cabinetry and a statement pendant light, while bespoke wardrobe joinery adds practical elegance to the interiors.

Refined bathrooms combine floating vanities, stone-look finishes and soft integrated lighting. An expansive covered terrace with decorative lighting provides an inviting setting for entertaining and enjoying the elevated outlook.
