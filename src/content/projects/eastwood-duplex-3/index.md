A striking stone-clad façade and clean architectural lines give this contemporary residence a commanding street presence. Inside, a double-height living space, expansive glazing and warm timber flooring create a bright, welcoming atmosphere. The sleek kitchen pairs a generous island with streamlined cabinetry and elegant pendant lighting.

Refined bathrooms feature twin basins, illuminated mirrors and textured finishes, while a sheltered swimming pool adds a distinctive retreat. Carefully considered materials and lighting bring warmth and sophistication throughout the home.
